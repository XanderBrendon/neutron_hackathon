  Ranking runs in three passes: shapes that fill the requested diameter beat shapes that don't, then fewer step bumps wins, then closer edge fit breaks the tie. Smoothness counts how many times the step profile goes back up as it descends the quadrant — zero means the arc thins monotonically, which is what reads as round. Edge fit is the RMS distance of every boundary pixel's centre from radius d/2, measured against the nominal circle rather than the offset one so it stays comparable across candidates. The top-ranked result is a starting point, not a verdict — below about 16 px there are few enough candidates that your eye beats any formula.
